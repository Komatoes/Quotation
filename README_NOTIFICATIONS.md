## 🔔 What Notifications Do

### Automatically Notifies You When:

1. **Customer Adds Comment** 💬
   - Notification created for all admin/staff
   - Click to navigate to quotation
   - See customer's comment immediately

2. **Quotation Approved** ✅
   - Green success notification
   - Shows who approved it
   - Client name included in message

3. **Quotation Rejected** ❌
   - Red rejection notification
   - Shows rejection reason
   - Can respond or take action

   - Project status changes
   - Progress updates
   - New quotations created

## 🆘 Need Help?

### Common Questions

**Q: How do I see notifications?**
A: Look for bell icon (🔔) in top-right corner of navbar. Click it to open dropdown.

**Q: Why don't I see notifications?**
A: You might need admin/staff role. Check you're logged in as admin.

**Q: How do I delete a notification?**
A: Click the delete icon (🗑️) on the notification or use "Clear All" button.

**Q: How often do notifications update?**
A: Every 30 seconds automatically. Or manually refresh the page.

**Q: Can I customize notification types?**
A: See `NOTIFICATIONS_SETUP.md` → NotificationHelper section

**Q: What happens when I click a notification?**
A: It marks as read and navigates to the related quotation automatically.
